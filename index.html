M.PHIL. RESEARCH · 2023 TO 2025

Which method fills the gaps best?

Comparative Analysis of Advanced Machine Learning Techniques and Statistical Methods for Missing Data Imputation: A Simulation Study

Designed and conducted a simulation study using R and Python to compare advanced machine learning techniques with traditional statistical methods for missing data imputation. The research evaluated imputation performance in terms of accuracy, bias and efficiency under different missingness mechanisms.
